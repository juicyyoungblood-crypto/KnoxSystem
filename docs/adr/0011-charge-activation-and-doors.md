# Charge is a key-press dash with strict stamina moodle gate

Charge activates on key press (not a passive sprint convert): dash forward, pay a large stamina cost up front, no cooldown. Any endurance/stamina moodle blocks use. XP uses the less expensive of “per zombie damaged” vs “sum of damage” — prefer per-zombie damaged when equal design value. World collision: may bash prebuilt/world doors at sufficient level; never bashes player-constructed doors. Zombies on the path take damage and are pushed aside.
