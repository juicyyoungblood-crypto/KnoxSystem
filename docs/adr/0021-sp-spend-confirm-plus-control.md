# SP spends use a pending cart with plus/minus and a bottom Confirm

Skill Points are never applied on the plus click alone. When unspent SP remains, plus controls appear (on Skills tab beside skill names like “Axe +”, and on System Tab beside Personal Stats). Plus stages an unconfirmed increase; minus appears for lines with pending increases and removes them. A single Confirm control at the bottom of the tab commits the pending cart. Prevents misclicks and allows multi-buy review before scarce SP is gone.
